Who I Am

I'm currently ranked the #1 collegiate 308-lb powerlifter in the country, with over 7 years of dedicated powerlifting experience. My journey began in high school, where I won two state championships and a national title, and it has since evolved into a lifelong passion for strength and performance. Over the years, I've trained under multiple coaching systems, gaining a deep understanding of different methodologies and what truly works for long-term progress. After overcoming a serious injury, I developed an even greater respect for smart, individualized programming and proper recovery — principles that I now bring into my coaching. Whether you're chasing a new PR or stepping on the platform for the first time, I'm here to help you train with purpose, stay healthy, and unlock your full potential.
